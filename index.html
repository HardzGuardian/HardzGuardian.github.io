About

This is my personal media library — a collection of movies, TV shows, anime, manga, novels, books, and tools I've gathered and organized over time.

Everything is sorted by category with cover art, filters, and a gallery view, making it easy to find and browse whatever I'm looking for.

Built for fun as a way to keep my collection organized and easy to browse — glad it turned into something genuinely usable, and I hope you enjoy it too.

Enjoy browsing! ♡

If you’d like to help sort photos, report issues, or suggest improvements, message me on Discord @hardzguardian Copied! .
Layout may not display well on mobile.

Usage

  • Use the left sidebar to browse categories and open a library page, or search for a specific title.
  • Once you're viewing a category's page, you can type a title into the search bar to quickly jump to that entry's cover.
  • Use the random button to open a random image from the current gallery.
  • Click any image to open the lightbox and view the full resolution version, and use the download button to save it.
  • In the lightbox, hold Ctrl and click on an image to open the magnifier, then use your scroll wheel to zoom in and out.
  • Keep an eye out for the cat wandering around the page — it'll chase your cursor if you move it nearby.

Buttons

  • Sort by Date: Toggle between ascending/descending date order.
  • Chronological / Recent: Toggle sorting between manually assigned dates (chronological) or by the most recently saved images. Useful for seeing new additions even if they're from earlier dates.
  • Grid / Masonry: Toggle between uniform grid or natural aspect‑ratio layout.
  • Filter: Open the filter panel to narrow entries by Status, Genre, or Rating.
